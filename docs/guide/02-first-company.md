# 2. Your first company

Each company is kept in its own local database on this PC. Live companies (open in TallyPrime) and
client backups restored on your PC are kept apart, even with the same name.

## 1. Open it in TallyPrime

Open the company (Alt+F3). In the control panel's **Add a company** section, click **Refresh
list** and pick it.

## 2. Record your authorisation

The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 applies to personal data in the books, and what
the AI reads is sent to your AI service provider. Before the first sync, the tool needs your
authorisation record for this financial year:

| Choose | When |
|---|---|
| Self-certification | You are a professional (CA, accountant) with the business's written authorisation |
| Owner's declaration | These are your own business's books |
| Self-certification + letter or email | As above, and you want a copy of the signed letter or email kept (encrypted) on this PC |

Read the notice and the declaration, type **I AGREE**, and click **Record and continue**.
The tool cannot check that a letter is genuine; the legal responsibility stays with you. You are
asked again each financial year.

## 3. Sync

Click **Sync now**. The tool reads the books month by month, one small request at a time, so
TallyPrime stays usable. Progress shows on the page. A large company takes a few minutes the first
time; later syncs read only what changed.

When it finishes, the company card shows:
- **Trial balance vs Tally: matches**. If not, see [9. Problems](09-problems.md); answers carry
  a warning until it matches.
- The number of vouchers and ledgers, and the period.
- The authorisation and when it needs renewing.

## Later

- **Sync now** brings in only what changed. The AI app also checks for changes before answering.
- **Full re-check** reads every month again (use it if something looks wrong).
- **Delete local data** removes this company's copy from your PC. Type the company name to confirm.
  Nothing in Tally changes.
